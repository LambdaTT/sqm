💬 Commentaries:

Completes the IAM permissions-domain alignment (IAM v1.3.0): re-enables permission enforcement
using the new tag-based permissioning standard.

---

✨ Added:

    ▸ Seed `dbseeds/1795316500_sqm-permissions.php`, registering the module's permission tags in
      `IAM_PERMISSION`: `sqm.entry.read` and `sqm.entry.manage`.

---

🔧 Changed:

    ▸ Replaced the entity/CRUD permission codes (`SQM_ENTRY` C/U) with tag-based permissions
      enforced via `iam/permission->requireAny()`. Creating an entry and changing its status now
      require `sqm.entry.manage`. The current-queue and entry listing endpoints remain public, as
      before.

    ▸ Module presence is now checked via `ModsManager::modExists('iam')`, and the disabled
      `validatePermissions()` reference was removed.
